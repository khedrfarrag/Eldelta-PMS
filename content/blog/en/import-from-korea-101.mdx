## Why import from Korea?
South Korea is a leader in electronics, automotive parts, chemicals, cosmetics, and machinery with competitive pricing and innovation.

## Key documents
- Commercial Invoice, Packing List
- Purchase Order/Contract
- Certificate of Origin (if preferences apply)
- B/L or AWB
- Export declaration (UNI-PASS)
- Product-specific conformity (KCs marks for certain categories; destination requirements as applicable)

## Process
1. Confirm HS codes and destination rules.
2. Check conformity (e.g., KCs for Korean market; destination rules like SASO/SFDA if importing into KSA).
3. Agree Incoterms and insurance.
4. Book shipment and verify B/L marks and weights.
5. Exporter files export via UNI-PASS.
6. Ship and track; prepare clearance docs for destination.

## Timelines & costs
- Docs: 2–5 business days
- Sea freight Korea→GCC: 12–25 days; Air: 3–5 days
- Conformity/testing (if any): 3–10 business days
Costs depend on route, volume, Incoterms, and tests.

## Risks
- HS misclassification
- Missing destination conformity (e.g., IECEE/SFDA)
- Freight adjustments due to volumetric weight

## Useful links
- Korea Customs Service (UNI-PASS): https://unipass.customs.go.kr
- KOTRA/KITA trade info: https://www.kita.org

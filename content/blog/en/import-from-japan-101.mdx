## Why import from Japan?
Japan offers high-quality electronics, automotive parts, machinery, specialized tools, and premium consumer goods with strong reliability and after-sales ecosystems.

## Key documents
- Commercial Invoice and Packing List
- Sales contract or Purchase Order
- Certificate of Origin (when preferential tariffs apply)
- Transport document (B/L or AWB)
- Export declaration from Japan (NACCS system)
- Product-specific conformity docs (electrical safety, RoHS-like requirements by destination)

## Steps to import
1. Product selection and HS code confirmation with your customs broker.
2. Verify destination-country regulations and any SASO/SABER/SFDA needs if importing into KSA.
3. Agree Incoterms (FOB Yokohama, CIF Jeddah, etc.).
4. Book shipment and obtain draft B/L; verify marks and weights.
5. Japanese exporter files export declaration through NACCS.
6. Cargo departs; track vessel/flight; arrange insurance.
7. Arrival and customs clearance at destination via local platform (e.g., FASAH in KSA).

## Timelines and indicative costs
- Sourcing & docs: 2–7 business days
- Sea freight Japan→GCC: ~14–28 days; Air: 3–6 days
- Conformity/testing (if needed): 3–10 business days
Costs vary by Incoterms, route, weight/volume, and product tests.

## Quality and compliance tips
- Confirm voltage/plug standards for electronics.
- Request factory test reports and warranties.
- Validate supplier via JETRO directories or trade references.

## Common risks
- HS misclassification leading to extra duties/delays
- Missing conformity for regulated goods in destination
- Under-declaration of weight/volume causing freight adjustments

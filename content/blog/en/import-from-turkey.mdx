# Complete Guide to Importing from Turkey 2024

Turkey is a bridge between Europe and Asia, offering tremendous opportunities for importers with European quality and competitive prices. In this comprehensive guide, we'll cover everything you need to know for successful importing from Turkey.

## 📋 Required Documents

### Turkish Company Documents
- **Vergi Numarası** (Turkish Tax Number)
- **Ticaret Sicil Kaydı** (Turkish Trade Registry)
- **İhracat İthalat Lisansı** (Export Import License)
- **Banka Hesabı** (Company bank account)
- **Şirket Kuruluş Belgesi** (Company formation certificate)

### Import Documents
- **Import License** (from Ministry of Commerce and Investment)
- **Certificate of Origin** (from Turkish manufacturer - certified)
- **Commercial Invoice** (in English)
- **Packing List** (detailed)
- **Bill of Lading** (from shipping company)
- **Inspection Certificate** (from certified inspection company)

### Customs Documents
- **Customs Declaration** (prepared by licensed customs agent)
- **Compliance Certificate** (for required products by type)
- **Health Certificate** (for food and medical products)
- **Safety Certificate** (for electronic products)

## ⏰ Detailed Timeline

### Phase 1: Research and Selection (1-2 weeks)
- **Week 1**: Finding suitable suppliers
  - Using B2B platforms like Turkishexporter and ExportHub
  - Contacting 5-10 potential suppliers
  - Requesting catalogs and initial prices

- **Week 2**: Supplier evaluation
  - Requesting samples from 3-5 suppliers
  - Quality inspection and specification compliance
  - Price and terms negotiation

### Phase 2: Order and Production (2-4 weeks)
- **Week 1**: Sending Purchase Order
  - Detailed specification definition
  - Payment and delivery terms agreement
  - Final contract signing

- **Week 2-3**: Production monitoring
  - Regular factory visits (optional)
  - Quality inspection during production
  - Ensuring specification compliance

- **Week 4**: Final pre-shipment inspection
  - Comprehensive goods inspection
  - Ensuring proper packaging
  - Issuing final inspection certificate

### Phase 3: Shipping and Receipt (1-2 weeks)
- **Week 1**: Shipping arrangement
  - Choosing shipping method (sea/air/land)
  - Container or aircraft booking
  - Shipping documents preparation

- **Week 2**: Shipping and receipt
  - Goods loading
  - Sea or air journey start
  - Goods arrival at port
  - Customs clearance
  - Goods receipt

## 💰 Detailed Expected Costs

### Product Costs (70-85% of total cost)
- **Product Price**: $8,000 - $40,000 depending on quantity
- **Packaging Cost**: 5-10% of product price
- **Sample Cost**: $40-200 per product
- **Inspection Fees**: $180-450 per inspection

### Shipping Costs
- **Sea Freight (FCL)**: $700-1,300 for 20ft container
- **Sea Freight (LCL)**: $7-14 per cubic meter
- **Air Freight**: $7-14 per kg
- **Land Freight**: $0.4-0.9 per kg
- **Port Fees**: $180-450

### Customs and Tax Costs
- **Customs Duties**: 5-15% depending on product type
- **Value Added Tax**: 15%
- **Customs Clearance Fees**: $90-280
- **Storage Fees**: $45-90 daily

### Additional Costs
- **Cargo Insurance**: 0.5-1% of goods value
- **Bank Fees**: 0.1-0.5% of transaction value
- **Translation Fees**: $45-180 per document
- **Certification Fees**: $18-45 per document

## 🚨 Common Risks and How to Avoid Them

### 1. Quality Issues
**Problem**: Products don't match samples or required specifications

**Solutions**:
- Request detailed samples before large orders
- Contract with certified inspection companies (SGS, Bureau Veritas)
- Include penalty clauses in contract
- Regular factory visits

**Cost**: $180-450 per inspection

### 2. Delivery Delays
**Problem**: Delays in production or shipping

**Solutions**:
- Include penalty clauses in contract
- Regular follow-up with supplier
- Cargo insurance
- Having backup plan

**Insurance**: $90-280 monthly

### 3. Customs Issues
**Problem**: Goods rejection or additional fees

**Solutions**:
- Ensure all documents are correct
- Consult licensed customs agent
- Know updated customs laws
- Prepare detailed product list

**Cost**: $90-280 to correct documents

### 4. Payment Issues
**Problem**: Supplier not receiving payment or transfer delays

**Solutions**:
- Use Letter of Credit (L/C)
- Verify bank details
- Use secure payment methods
- Split payment into stages

**Cost**: 0.5-1% of transaction value

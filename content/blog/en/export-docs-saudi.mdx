## Quick overview (When do you need each document?)

| Document | Mandatory | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial Invoice | Yes | Unified data: value, Incoterms, payment terms |
| Packing List | Yes | Number of packages, weights, dimensions |
| Certificate of Origin | Often | Issued by Chamber of Commerce in exporting country |
| Bill of Lading / Air Waybill | Yes | Issued by the carrier (sea/air) |
| Export Customs Declaration | Yes | Proof of exit from country of export |
| Conformity/Safety Certificates (SASO/IECEE/SFDA) | Product-based | IECEE for electricals, SFDA for food/drugs |
| Logistics & service invoices | Yes | Freight, insurance, handling |

## Core documents in detail

### Commercial Invoice
- Exporter/importer details, tax numbers if any.
- Detailed description, HS code, country of origin, quantities.
- Incoterms (FOB/CIF/DDP), currency, payment terms, stamp/signature.

### Packing List
- Number of packages, packaging type, net/gross weight, dimensions.
- Package numbering and marks matching the B/L.

### Certificate of Origin
- Issued by the Chamber of Commerce.
- Some cases require consular attestation.

### Transport document (B/L or AWB)
- Ports/airports of loading and discharge.
- Prefer negotiable B/L when using L/C.

### Export customs declaration
- Evidence of exit from origin, often required in KSA clearance file.

## Product-dependent documents

### Electrical & Electronics
- IECEE conformity certificate for regulated products.
- Product registration on SABER platform by the KSA importer.

### Food, beverages, supplements
- SFDA requirements: health certificates, compliant food labels.

### Cosmetics & detergents
- Product listing with SFDA (as applicable).

### Toys, children products, textiles
- SASO safety standards and accredited testing reports.

## Step-by-step process
1. Prepare commercial docs (Invoice + Packing + PO/contract).
2. Verify product conformity needs (SASO/IECEE/SFDA) with importer and update SABER when needed.
3. Book shipment (sea/air) and obtain draft B/L or AWB.
4. Perform export clearance in origin and issue export declaration.
5. Ship cargo and coordinate arrival with importer in KSA.
6. Importer completes KSA clearance via FASAH/SABER.

## Time & indicative costs
- Commercial docs prep: 1–2 business days.
- Certificate of Origin: same day to 2 business days.
- Conformity certificates (if any): 3–10 business days.
- Sea freight: 10–30 days; Air: 2–6 days.
- Government/service fees vary by product, quantity, and route.

> Note: figures are indicative; confirm with importer and logistics provider.

## Common mistakes & risks
- HS code mismatch between invoice and declarations.
- Missing IECEE/SFDA for regulated goods leading to rejection.
- Weight/quantity discrepancies vs. B/L.
- Unclear Incoterms causing cost disputes.

## FAQ
### Is Certificate of Origin always required?
Not always, but commonly needed to define origin and preferences.

### Who handles SABER?
The KSA importer, coordinating with exporter and accredited labs.
